Study Methodology

Lettuce plants were grown in Veggie hardware on the ISS and in ground control chambers at KSC. Samples were harvested, frozen, and returned to Earth. Nutritional analysis included ICP-OES for elemental composition, Folin-Ciocalteu for phenolics, pH differential for anthocyanins, and ORAC for antioxidant capacity.

ML Pipeline

Data preprocessing included standardization and handling missing values. PCA was used for dimensionality reduction. A Random Forest classifier was trained to distinguish flight from ground samples, and feature importance was extracted.

Data Provenance (FAIR)

All data is sourced from NASA OSDR OSD-745 following FAIR principles.
